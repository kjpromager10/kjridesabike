Bolt grade & torque reference

Engineering reference for common fastener grades and typical torque ranges on Indian performance motorcycles. Cross-referenced from publicly available service manuals.

Important: This is an educational reference only. Torque values shown are general ranges compiled from publicly available service manuals and are not a substitute for your manufacturer's service manual. Always verify torque specs from your bike's official service documentation before any work. Incorrect torque can cause component failure, injury, or death.
